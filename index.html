What is IslandSMP?

IslandSMP is a fun and friendly Public Minecraft Survival Server.

How do I join IslandSMP?

If you want to join IslandSMP on Java, click "Add Server", then type IslandSMP.apexmc.co for the IP.

For Bedrock, click "Add Server", then type for the IP IslandSMP.apexmc.co and for the port type 25756.

How do I check my money?

To check your money, type /Bal or /Baltop. You can also type /Shop and look for the sunflower icon to see your balance.

You can also type /balance or /balancetop to check your money!

Spawners

Pig Spawner: The Pig Spawner has a 70% chance to generate Pork Chops to eat or sell.

Bat Spawner: The Bat Spawner has an 85% chance to generate Spider Eyes to sell.

Spider Spawner: The Spider Spawner has a 58% chance to generate Spider Eyes and an 88% chance to generate String to craft or sell.

Zombie Spawner: The Zombie Spawner has a 66% chance to generate Rotten Flesh, a 1% chance to generate Iron Ingots, a 24% chance to generate Carrots, a 6% chance to generate Potatoes, and a 45% chance to generate Golden Ingots. There is also a 0.1% chance to generate a Zombie Head.

Skeleton Spawner: The Skeleton Spawner has a 79% chance to generate a Bone, a 72% chance to generate an Arrow, an 18% chance to generate a Bow, and a 0.1% chance to generate a Skeleton Skull.

Enderman Spawner: The Enderman Spawner has an 65% chance to generate an Ender Pearl and an 80% chance to generate End Stone.

Piglin Spawner: The Piglin Spawner has an 8% chance to generate a Golden Sword, an 8% chance to generate a Crossbow, and a 70% chance to generate Gold Ingots.

Crates

Ender Crate. The Ender crate is a special crate you can only open with Keys of Ender. The chances are:

Stats of the Ender Crate
  • Ender Pearl 15% chance
  • End Stone x5 15% chance
  • End Stone Bricks x5 15% chance
  • Obsidian x5 14% chance
  • Ender Chest 13% chance
  • End Rod 12% chance
  • Eye of Ender 10% chance
  • Ender Helmet 2% chance
  • Ender Chestplate 1% chance
  • Ender Leggings 1% chance
  • Ender Boots 2% chance
  • Ender Sword 1% chance

Finally, the rarest item you can get is an Enderman Spawner with a 0.63% chance.
